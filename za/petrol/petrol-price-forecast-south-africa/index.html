Why are prices rising?

The historic hike on April 1st, 2026, is driven by a surge in Brent Crude oil (now over $100/bbl) following Middle East tensions and the weakening of the Rand. Additionally, the annual General Fuel Levy and Road Accident Fund (RAF) tax adjustments add 21c/litre to the total increase.

Key Data Points for April 2026

  • Supply Constraints The primary driver is the international cost of refined petroleum, which has surged due to supply constraints.
  • Tax Hike: A confirmed 21c/litre (Petrol) and 23c/litre (Diesel) tax increase is included in the forecast above.
